# Data Deletion Policy for FitOwn

**Effective Date:** October 05, 2026

At **FitOwn**, we deeply care about your data privacy. Because FitOwn is a **100% local, offline-first application**, we **DO NOT** store, collect, or manage any of your personal, health, or fitness data on external servers. Everything is encrypted on your device via a secure local SQLite database powered by native Rust execution.

---

## How to Delete Your Data

Since all data remains exclusively on your device, you have complete ownership and control over it. You can completely and permanently delete all your data at any time by following either of these steps:

### 1. In-App Deletion
* Open **FitOwn** on your device.
* Navigate to the **Settings** tab.
* Click the **"Delete All Local Data"** button. 
* This will instantly and permanently wipe the encrypted SQLite database from your phone's storage.

### 2. Device Uninstallation
* Go to your Android System Settings.
* Find **FitOwn** under your Applications list.
* Select **Uninstall**.
* This action automatically purges all locally stored files, workouts, supplement logs, and clinical biomarkers associated with the app.

---

## Data Retention & Backups

* **Third-Party Services (License Status):** If you purchased the Premium version, transaction data is processed securely through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. This data is maintained according to Google Play’s terms to ensure your lifetime license status can be restored if you switch devices.
* **Cloud Backups (Google Drive):** If you voluntarily utilized the Google Drive backup feature, you can delete your backup file at any time directly from your personal Google Drive account under the **"App Data"** management section. FitOwn does not keep any copies or logs of your backups.

There are no additional retention periods. Once you delete the data from your device, it is **gone forever** and cannot be recovered.
